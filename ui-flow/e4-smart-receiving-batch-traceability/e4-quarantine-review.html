Quarantine routing is enforced by the system, not by convention. Every override — and every refused attempt — is written to the append-only audit log with the actor, timestamp and full justification (5-year retention per Rwanda FDA Law N°003/2018). An override is never edited away; a mistake is corrected by a new, separately recorded action.
