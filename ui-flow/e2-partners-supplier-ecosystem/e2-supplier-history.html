Picker and Driver roles are redirected to Access Denied and the attempt is written to the audit log. Roles that may view but not export never see the Export button.

Supplier history is part of batch traceability — it links each received batch back to the supplier that produced it. Events are append-only and never hard-deleted, and every Audit Mode export writes supplier_history.exported to the audit log (5-year retention per Rwanda FDA Law N°003/2018).
